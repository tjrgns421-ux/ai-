# EP02 Roanoke (Lost Colony) — sources, fact check, image credits

## Fact check (2026-11-26)

| Claim in script | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ~115 colonists landed 1587; governor John White | Standard accounts (Wikipedia "Roanoke Colony", NPS) | "about 115" |
| Virginia Dare born Aug 1587, first English child born in the Americas | Same | |
| White returned Aug 1590 (Virginia Dare's 3rd birthday), settlement empty, houses taken down, palisade, chests dug up, "CRO" on a tree, "CROATOAN" on a post, no cross | White's 1590 narrative, printed in Hakluyt, Principal Navigations (1600) | Script says "gatepost"; White: a post at the palisade entrance |
| Storm turned White's ships back | White's narrative | |
| 1587–1589 worst 3-year drought in 800-year bald cypress record | Stahle et al. 1998, Science 280:564–567, doi:10.1126/science.280.5363.564 | Chart on screen is schematic (shape only), labelled |
| Strachey reported Powhatan's claim of killing English living with the Chesapeake, around Jamestown's arrival | Strachey, Historie of Travell into Virginia Britannia (c. 1612) | Presented as secondhand |
| 2012: patch on White's map (British Museum) hides a fort symbol at head of Albemarle Sound; First Colony Foundation found English pottery (Border ware) at "Site X", announced 2015 | NPS; First Colony Foundation; WUNC/History.com coverage | Critics: trade could explain the pottery |
| Horton & Dawson report English objects on Hatteras (gun parts, sword piece, writing slate) | Dawson, The Lost Colony and Hatteras Island (2020); press coverage | |
| 2025: hammerscale announced from Hatteras in soil dated to the period; argued Croatoan didn't forge iron | Live Science, WHRO, Fox News coverage (May–June 2025) | No peer-reviewed paper found; Luccketti (Site X) skeptical — script says "as far as I could find" |
| DNA link not yet shown | Coverage through 2026 | |

Meter numbers are the narrator's judgement, said out loud as judgement.

Sources
