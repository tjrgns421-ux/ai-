# EP01 Voynich — sources, fact check, image credits

## Fact check (run 2026-11-23)

| Claim | Result | Script change |
|---|---|---|
| Marci letter repeats claim Rudolf II paid 600 ducats | Confirmed. Marci passes on what Raphael Mnišovský told him (that Rudolf II bought it for 600 ducats); it is hearsay, 20+ years old. "Repeats a claim" is accurate. | none |
| Ink/paint consistent with period (McCrone) | Confirmed at summary level: 2009 McCrone Associates analysis for the Beinecke found iron-gall ink, azurite and other period materials; some researchers criticise the iron-gall identification. | "studies of the ink and paint" → "a 2009 analysis of the ink and paint" (one analysis, dated) |
| Rugg, Cardan grille, Cryptologia 2004 | Confirmed: "An Elegant Hoax? A Possible Solution to the Voynich Manuscript", Cryptologia 28(1), Jan 2004 (Nature news Dec 2003, SciAm 2004). Grille dates to c. 1550, so "available in the 1500s" is right. | "psychologist Gordon Rugg" → "researcher Gordon Rugg" (his role is usually given as computer scientist/psychologist; neutral wording avoids a wrong title) |
| Davis 2020: five scribes | Confirmed: "How Many Glyphs and How Many Scribes? Digital Paleography and the Voynich Manuscript", Manuscript Studies 5(1), 2020, pp. 164–180. | none |
| Words repeat 2–3× in a row | Confirmed (standard observation, e.g. "qokedy qokedy dal qokedy qokedy" in the EVA transcription). On screen shown with an EVA example and labelled as transcription. | none |

Schematic visuals are labelled "schematic" on screen: Zipf chart (shape, not measured counts), keyword strip, five-scribe glyphs, grille table, Naibbe method, sheet unbinding.
