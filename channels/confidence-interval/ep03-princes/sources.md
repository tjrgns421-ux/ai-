# EP03 Princes in the Tower — sources, fact check, image credits

## Fact check (2026-11-26)

| Claim in script | Basis | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Edward IV died April 1483; Edward V aged 12, Richard (Duke of York) 9 | Standard accounts | Edward V b. Nov 1470, Richard b. Aug 1473 |
| Boys declared illegitimate in June; Richard crowned July 1483 | Standard accounts | Coronation 6 July 1483 |
| Mancini in London 1483, left July, report finished Dec 1483, found in Lille 1934 | Mancini, De occupatione regni Anglie; Armstrong ed. | On-screen quote: Armstrong translation |
| Edward's daily confession "because he believed that death was facing him", via his doctor (John Argentine) | Mancini (Armstrong trans.) | Shown as a quote from the translation |
| Rumours of their death by autumn 1483; Buckingham's rebellion October 1483 | Crowland Chronicle; standard accounts | |
| Richard never produced the boys in public | Absence of any record | Presented as "not proof" |
| 1674 skeletons of two children found under a stair at the White Tower; Charles II had them buried in Westminster Abbey | Westminster Abbey; Historic Royal Palaces | Urn placed 1678 |
| 1933 examination (Tanner & Wright): two children of about the right ages; sex and date not determinable; modern testing incl. DNA refused | Westminster Abbey; Wikipedia | |
| More (c. 1513) names Tyrrell, Forest and Dighton; bodies "at the stair foot" | More, History of King Richard III | |
| Thornton: probable sons of Miles Forest at the Tudor court as More's possible source | Thornton, "More on a Murder", History (online Dec 2020, issue 2021), doi:10.1111/1468-229X.13100 | Script says "published in 2021" |
| Langley 2023: archival documents argued to show both princes alive after 1485; Gelderland statement linked to "Richard, Duke of York" (Perkin Warbeck, executed 1499); three Dutch researchers distanced themselves | Langley, The Princes in the Tower (2023); NOS and press coverage | |
| Margaret Capell's 1516 will: gold chain that had belonged to Edward V; her sister Anne Arundell married James Tyrrell | Thornton, "Sir William Capell and a Royal Chain: The Afterlives (and Death) of King Edward V", History 109 (2024) | Will proved 1522; chain lost |

Meter numbers are the narrator's judgement, said out loud as judgement.
