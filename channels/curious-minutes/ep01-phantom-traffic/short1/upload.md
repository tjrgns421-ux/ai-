```
A Traffic Jam With No Crash 🚗 #shorts
```

## 설명
```
In 2008, researchers put 22 cars on an empty 230 m circle and asked the drivers to keep a steady speed. A jam formed anyway.
The full 9-minute video (why the jam moves backward, and what one calm car can do) is on the channel.

3D scene: a simulation recreating the experiment, partly sped up (3.5×).
Source: Sugiyama et al. (2008), "Traffic jams without bottlenecks", New Journal of Physics 10, 033001

#science #traffic #physics
```
