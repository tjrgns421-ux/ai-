Wording rules for the script
- Use "about 30 km/h (about 19 mph)" and "about 20 km/h"; these are the paper's approximate values.
- The 40% / 15% are **upper bounds in a test ring**, not highway results: say "up to … on a test track".
- Do not claim a driver can single-handedly clear a jam on a real highway; the evidence is the ring experiment.
