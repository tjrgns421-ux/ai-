## 설명
```
In December 2021, China's Yutu 2 rover spotted a cube-shaped blur on the far side of the Moon. Its own team joked it might be the hut of alien pioneers. After about a month of driving, the close-up came back: a small, round rock that looked like a rabbit. The team nicknamed it jade rabbit, which is exactly what Yutu means.
Verdict: solved. It was a rock.

The other five strange things found on the Moon (the gel, the Moon that rang, rust, swirls and the two faces) are in the full video on the channel.
```
