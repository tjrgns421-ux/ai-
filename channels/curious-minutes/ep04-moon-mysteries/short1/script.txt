You'd never expect a hut on the Moon.
But in 2021, a rover drove a month to reach one. What was it, actually?
China's Yutu 2 rover spotted a cube-shaped blur on the far side.
Its own team joked it might be the hut of alien pioneers.
So the rover drove there.
The close-up showed a rock. Small, round, and shaped like a rabbit.
The team named it jade rabbit. Which is exactly what Yutu means.
That's one of six strange things found on the Moon. The rest are on the channel.
