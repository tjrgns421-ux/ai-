## 설명
```
On Racetrack Playa in Death Valley, rocks leave long trails across a dry lake bed. For almost a century, nobody saw one move. In the winter of 2013–14, a research team put GPS trackers in rocks and set up time-lapse cameras, and on December 20, 2013, they watched more than sixty rocks move at once. Thin sheets of ice, a few millimeters thick, were pushed by light wind and shoved the rocks across the soft mud. Verdict: solved.

Five more strange places are in the full video on the channel.

Sources: Wikipedia, "Sailing stones"; Norris et al., PLOS ONE (2014), "Sliding Rocks on Racetrack Playa, Death Valley National Park: First Observation of Rocks in Motion"
```
