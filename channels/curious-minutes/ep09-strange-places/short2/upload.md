## 설명

In Turkmenistan's Karakum Desert, the Darvaza gas crater, the "door to hell", is about 60 to 70 meters wide and has been burning for decades. The usual story: in 1971, a Soviet drilling rig hit a cavern of natural gas, the ground collapsed, and engineers set it on fire. But the records from that time are missing, and some local geologists say the crater formed in the 1960s and was only lit in the 1980s. Verdict: best guess.
