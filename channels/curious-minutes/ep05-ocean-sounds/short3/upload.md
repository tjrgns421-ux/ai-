## 설명
```
In 1989, scientists at Woods Hole heard a whale calling at 52 hertz in the North Pacific. Blue and fin whales call at around 15 to 25 hertz. Over 12 years of tracking, from California to the Aleutian Islands, they found only one voice like it each season. A malformation? A hybrid? Nobody has confirmed it. Verdict: still unknown.

Five more strange ocean sounds, with the real recordings, are in the full video on the channel.

Audio: NOAA Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory (sped up 10x, as published). 3D scenes are illustrations.
Sources: WHOI Oceanus (2005), "A Lone Voice Crying in the Watery Wilderness"; Watkins et al. (2004), Deep-Sea Research I
```
