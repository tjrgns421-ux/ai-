## 설명

In 1997, NOAA's underwater microphones caught a sound so loud that sensors more than 5,000 km apart picked it up. People wondered about a giant animal. Then in 2008, NOAA tracked iceberg A53a breaking apart near South Georgia, and its icequakes looked very similar. The Bloop was ice, cracking. Verdict: solved.

Five more strange ocean sounds, with the real recordings, are in the full video on the channel.
