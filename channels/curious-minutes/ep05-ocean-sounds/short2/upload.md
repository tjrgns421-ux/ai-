## 설명
```
Submarine crews in the Southern Ocean first heard it in the 1960s: a repeating sound that reminded them of a duck. For about 50 years, nobody knew what made it. In 2013, scientists attached suction-cup recorders to Antarctic minke whales, and one tag caught the sound. The Bio-Duck is a whale. Verdict: solved.

Five more strange ocean sounds, with the real recordings, are in the full video on the channel.

Audio: NOAA Fisheries (real speed). 3D scenes are illustrations.
```
