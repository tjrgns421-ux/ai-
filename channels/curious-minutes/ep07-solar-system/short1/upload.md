In July 1976, NASA's Viking 1 orbiter photographed a 1.5-kilometer rock formation on Mars that looked like a human face. Even NASA's caption at the time said the face was formed by shadows. In 2001 and 2007, sharper cameras on Mars Global Surveyor and Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter showed an ordinary eroded hill. Verdict: solved.
