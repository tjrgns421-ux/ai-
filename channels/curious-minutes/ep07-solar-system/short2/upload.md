## 설명

In 2017, ʻOumuamua became the first object confirmed to come from interstellar space. As it left the Sun, it sped up slightly, like a comet releasing gas, but it showed no visible cloud and no tail. In 2023, scientists suggested in Nature that hydrogen, made by cosmic rays in its ice and released when it warmed up, gave it a gentle push. Verdict: best guess. It's long gone, so we can't check.
