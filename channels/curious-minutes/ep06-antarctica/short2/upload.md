## 설명
```
Blood Falls pours out of Taylor Glacier in Antarctica, where the average temperature is about −17 °C. Radar found a hidden network of rivers and a lake under the glacier, full of very salty, iron-rich water. Salt keeps it from freezing, and when the iron meets the air it turns red, like rust. Verdict: solved.

Five more Antarctic mysteries are in the full video on the channel.

3D scenes are illustrations. Photo: Peter Rejcek / U.S. National Science Foundation (public domain). Source: National Geographic, "What's really in Antarctica's mysterious Blood Falls"
```
