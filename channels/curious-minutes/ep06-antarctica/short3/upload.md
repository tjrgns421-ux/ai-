## 설명
```
In 2016 and 2017, a hole nearly twice the size of Wales opened in the Antarctic sea ice, far out at sea over an underwater mountain called Maud Rise. In 2024, scientists found the missing piece: currents and wind brought in extra salt, which kept warm water mixing up to the surface, so the hole stayed open. Verdict: solved.

Five more Antarctic mysteries are in the full video on the channel.

3D scenes are illustrations. Source: University of Southampton (May 1, 2024); Narayanan et al., Science Advances (2024)

#science #antarctica #ocean
```
