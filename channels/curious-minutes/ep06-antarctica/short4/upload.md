Some say the 1513 Piri Reis map shows the coast of Antarctica without its ice. The claim came from writer Charles Hapgood in the 1960s. But Piri Reis built his map from older maps, and the coast at the bottom fits South America, bent along the edge of the page. Verdict: solved.

Five more Antarctic mysteries are in the full video on the channel.
