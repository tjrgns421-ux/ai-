## 설명

In August 2023, NOAA's Okeanos Explorer found a smooth golden dome stuck to a rock about 3,300 meters deep in the Gulf of Alaska. Scientists guessed a sponge, a coral, even an egg case, but on the ship they could only say it was biological. In April 2026, NOAA announced the answer: it was once part of the base of a giant deep-sea anemone, Relicanthus daphneae. Verdict: solved.

Five more deep sea mysteries are in the full video on the channel.
