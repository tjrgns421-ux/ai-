## 설명
```
In 2022, NOAA's Voyage to the Ridge expedition found several sets of holes in straight-ish lines on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge seafloor, about 2,540 meters deep. NOAA said they look almost human made, but little piles of sediment around them suggest something dug them out. Sediment from fresher holes gave no clues, and similar holes were seen in the region back in 2004. Verdict: still unknown.

Five more deep sea mysteries are in the full video on the channel.
```
