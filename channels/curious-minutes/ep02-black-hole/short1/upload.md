Near a small black hole, tidal forces can rip you apart before you reach the edge. In a giant one like the one NASA simulated in 2024, you cross the edge in one piece, and spaghettification comes 12.8 seconds later.
The full 9-minute trip (what your friend sees, the real star that got torn apart, and the astronaut who comes home 36 minutes younger) is on the channel.

3D scenes: illustrations based on NASA's simulation. The countdown is sped up.
Source: NASA Goddard (May 6, 2024), "New NASA Black Hole Visualization Takes Viewers Beyond the Brink"
